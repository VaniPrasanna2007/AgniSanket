Supported Classification Taxonomy

Physics-grounded anomaly classes and satellite verification thresholds
Possible Industrial Thermal Event
Industrial Heat Source

Persistent thermal radiance emitted within 2 km of registered steel mills, cement kilns, brickworks, petrochemical refineries, or industrial complexes.

Threshold: Dist ≤ 2.0 km to OSM industry or Max FRP ≥ 25.0 MW with high recurrence.
Possible Vegetation / Agricultural Fire
Biomass / Agricultural Fire

Seasonal crop stubble combustion, agricultural residue burning, or wilderness wildfire fronts detected outside registered industrial zones.

Threshold: Dist > 10.0 km from heavy industry with multi-day persistence or high single-pass FRP.
Filtered False Positive
Filtered Noise

High-albedo solar reflections, industrial metallic roof glare, cloud edges, or single-pass transient sensor artifacts rejected by spatial coherence.

Threshold: NASA FIRMS confidence < 30% with zero multi-sensor thermal persistence.
Uncertain / Insufficient Evidence
Observation Obscured

Thermal signatures detected under dense cloud cover (> 70%) or where synchronous Landsat-9/Sentinel-2 STAC passes are temporarily unavailable.

Threshold: Cloud cover exceeds 70% or satellite pass temporal match quality is low.

About ML Insights & AI Safety Governance

Explainability guarantees, defense reliability, and zero-synthetic data commitment
Zero-Synthetic Data Policy

Models are trained strictly on verified human analyst annotations and real satellite radiance measurements. No synthetic fire records or hallucinated distributions are ever introduced.

TreeSHAP Mathematical Attribution

Every inference includes exact game-theoretic Shapley attributions, ensuring defense and emergency responders understand precisely why an anomaly was classified as industrial vs agricultural.

Active Learning Retraining

Continuous feedback queues capture analyst corrections in production. When sample counts reach the threshold (≥ 5 samples), administrators can trigger automated model retraining with zero downtime.

Technical Architecture: AgniSanket ML Governance

1. TreeSHAP Feature Attribution Theory

TreeSHAP computes exact Shapley values from cooperative game theory for tree ensembles in polynomial time $O(TLD^2)$. For an anomaly feature vector $x$, the attribution $\phi_i$ satisfies efficiency ($\sum \phi_i = f(x) - E[f(x)]$), symmetry, and additivity across all 100 decision trees.

2. Physics-Based Grounding Feature Engineering

Rather than black-box imagery alone, the model leverages physical thermal radiative power (FRP in MW) combined with Euclidean proximity to registered OpenStreetMap industrial facilities. This prevents confusing legal industrial flares with illegal forest encroachment fires.

3. Human-in-the-Loop Active Learning Pipeline

When analysts review clusters in the forensic investigation drawer, their "Confirm" or "Reject" decisions are saved to the persistent FeedbackLog table. The system continuously tracks sample count and allows authorized Administrators to generate calibrated model versions with version-tagged metrics.
